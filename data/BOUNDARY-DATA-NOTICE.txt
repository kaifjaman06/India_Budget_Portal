India district map data attribution

The bundled ../india-districts.js is an adapted derivative of the India
ADM2 (district) and ADM1 (state/Union Territory) boundary data distributed
by geoBoundaries: https://www.geoboundaries.org/

ADM2 district boundaries:
- Boundary ID: IND-ADM2-76128533
- Boundary year: 2021
- Source: Pathways Data Pvt. Ltd., Local Government Directory
- License: Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0
- License text: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- Dataset: https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/main/releaseData/gbOpen/IND/ADM2

ADM1 state and Union Territory boundaries, used to associate ADM2 districts
with their state or Union Territory:
- Boundary ID: IND-ADM1-1811400
- Boundary year: 2011
- Source: DataMeet India community, Election Commission of India
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 India (CC BY 2.5 IN)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/in/
- Dataset: https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/main/releaseData/gbOpen/IND/ADM1

The source geometry was simplified upstream, projected to an SVG viewBox,
and bundled as clickable paths. Maharashtra district labels are aligned with
the budget catalogue, including Ahmadnagar/Ahilyanagar,
Aurangabad/Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Bid/Beed, Buldana/Buldhana,
Gondiya/Gondia, Mumbai/Mumbai City, Osmanabad/Dharashiv, and
Raigarh/Raigad. Administrative boundaries are illustrative and do not
represent budget data or legal determinations of boundaries.
